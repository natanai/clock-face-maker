Classroom Clock Card Maker

Create accurate printable analog clock labels for visual schedules.

Make clock cards

Type one schedule item per line. Use Activity 7:00, Activity - 7:00, or just 7:00. The divider is optional.

Examples: Arrival 7:00, Breakfast - 8:30, Choice Time 9:00, or 10:15 AM.

Quick layout
Readable sizing

Cards keep overflow hidden so larger text stays inside each printed label. If text is clipped, choose a larger card or smaller font.

Advanced options
Display details

Force AM/PM labels requires accurate AM/PM input. Lines without AM/PM are marked so you can fix them.

Printable cards

Cards are sized in inches for print. On phones, scroll sideways to inspect the page preview.

All clocks are drawn as programmatic SVG in your browser. No data leaves your device.

Classroom Clock Card Maker

Create accurate printable analog clock labels for visual schedules.

Make clock cards

Type one schedule item per line. Use Activity 7:00, Activity - 7:00, 7:00 - 8:25 Activity, or just 7:00.

Examples: Arrival 7:00, Breakfast - 8:30, 9:00 - 9:30 Morning Meeting, or 10:15 AM.

Quick layout

Half-sheet and full-sheet presets automatically use one card across the page. Half sheet prints two cards on each portrait Letter page; full sheet prints one card on each landscape Letter page.

Readable sizing

The larger ranges let you scale the clock and text for half-sheet and full-sheet cards. Cards keep overflow hidden; if anything is clipped, reduce the clock or font size until the preview fits.

Advanced options
Display details

Force AM/PM labels requires accurate AM/PM input. Lines without AM/PM are marked so you can fix them.

Use these to match nearby curriculum materials while keeping the clock black-and-white and mathematically accurate.

Printable cards

Cards are sized in inches for print. On phones, scroll sideways to inspect the page preview.

All clocks are drawn as programmatic SVG in your browser. No data leaves your device.
